A setback journal

For iPhone. Free.

Log a setback in a few words, the moment it happens or later in the day. Your coach, an AI, recommends a way of seeing it, written for exactly what happened. You grade how you took it, and over the weeks you see how you meet setbacks and how fast you let them go.

Your journal stays on your phone. There are no ads, no tracking and no scores.
